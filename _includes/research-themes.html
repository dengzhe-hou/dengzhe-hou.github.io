Research themes

AI4Brain

AI for understanding the brain

Reliable decoding and automated analysis of EEG, and understanding self-initiated attention through EEG, eye tracking, and computational modeling.

  • Preprocessing instability
  • Per-trial uncertainty
  • Feature attribution

Brain4AI

Brain and cognitive science for understanding and improving AI

Drawing on cognitive neuroscience to understand, evaluate, and improve AI systems, especially large language models.

  • Working memory
  • Cumulative state tracking
  • Cognitive ability structure

Connecting these directions, my current work explores aligning brain signals (EEG, fMRI) with representations in large language and vision-language models.

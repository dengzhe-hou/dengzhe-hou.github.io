Cognitive EEG analysis involves a long chain of analytic decisions, and the freedom in that chain is exactly what makes results hard to trust. CogEEGAgent is an LLM agent that carries out such analyses autonomously while keeping every step grounded in executable code, and adds selection-aware verification so that the analytic choices themselves are examined rather than silently optimized for a favourable outcome.

Invited talk in Open Forum OF7, "テクノロジーの質的進化と組織統制" (Qualitative Evolution of Technology and Organizational Control), at the 44th Annual Conference of the Robotics Society of Japan, Kanazawa University Kakuma Campus.

The forum covers three emerging technology domains, Agentic AI, Physical AI and neurotechnology, each carrying a distinct risk structure that calls for governance designed in advance rather than retrofitted. This session covers the neurotechnology domain.

Organized by Tohoku University and NTT DATA Group.

**Speakers:** Dengzhe Hou, Michael Zielewski
